About Our Service

Get Digital Sign facilitates the application process for Digital Signature Certificates (DSCs). DSCs are issued by CCA-licensed Certifying Authorities under the Information Technology Act, 2000. We assist you with selecting the correct certificate type, collecting the required information, coordinating identity verification, and arranging delivery of the USB token containing your certificate.

Eligibility & Accurate Information

You must provide accurate, current, and complete information when applying for a Digital Signature Certificate. You are responsible for ensuring that the name, PAN, organization details, and any other information you submit match your official identity documents. Incorrect or mismatched information may delay or prevent issuance of your certificate.

Certificate Issuance Process

Certificates are issued only after successful identity verification, which may include video-based KYC as required by applicable Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA) guidelines. Processing times mentioned on this website (for example, "30 minutes to 2 hours") are estimates and may vary depending on document verification, certificate type, and circumstances beyond our control.

User Responsibilities

  • Keep your USB token and its password/PIN secure at all times.
  • Do not share your Digital Signature Certificate credentials with anyone.
  • Report a lost, stolen, or compromised USB token to us immediately so the certificate can be revoked.
  • Use your Digital Signature Certificate only for lawful purposes, in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000.
