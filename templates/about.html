About the Company

Get Digital Sign was established in 2012 with the goal of making the Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) application process simple, secure, and accessible.

Over the years, we have issued more than 100,000 Digital Signature Certificates and built long-term relationships with over 500 Chartered Accountants, professionals, and businesses across India.

We specialize in providing Class 3 DSC for Income Tax, GST, and MCA filings, ICEGATE digital signatures for import-export businesses, DGFT certificates for foreign trade documentation, and tender DSC certificates for government bidding portals.

Our focus is on fast processing, expert support, and ensuring that every certificate issued is fully compliant with the IT Act 2000.
